Bugs won't dare. Enjoy every moment.

FendOff is your trusted partner in mosquito protection. Our range of repellent sprays is designed to meet the varied needs of all our customers — choose from formulations with natural ingredients or effective DEET options, suitable for both kids and adults. Whether you're looking for everyday protection or preparing for an adventure to malaria-prone regions with our powerful 50% DEET spray, FendOff keeps you and your family safe and bite-free.

The Range

Five formulations, one standard of protection

All FendOff variants repel mosquitoes, ticks, and sandflies that may carry dengue fever, chikungunya, Zika virus, Lyme disease, and West Nile virus.

FendOff Ultra

30% DEET

Optimal strength for ultimate protection — 30% DEET provides adequate protection under most conditions. Available in compact 100ml and family 250ml.

FendOff Max

50% DEET

Maximum recommended strength, the first choice when travelling to malaria-prone areas. Compact 100ml, ideal for travel, hiking, backpacking, fishing and hunting.

FendOff Junior

10% DEET

A mild formula for children, giving up to 4 hours of protection when they are outdoors. 100ml pump spray.

FendOff Natural

DEET-FREE

A natural essential-oil based formula that repels mosquitoes, gnats and other biting insects. Mild and pleasantly fragrant.

FendOff DUO

2 IN ONE CREAM

Repellent and afterbite soothing cream in one. DEET-free, mild and pleasantly fragrant.

Using DEET safely: DEET can be applied to exposed skin, clothing, socks, and shoes. Avoid using it on a child's face, under clothing, on cuts or irritated skin, or on the hands of young children. Always read the label and follow the directions for use.
